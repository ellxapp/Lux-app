Luxembourg Approach

Work the approach position at Luxembourg Airport for 40 minutes. Vector arrivals onto the ILS, climb departures out on their SIDs and keep everyone separated.

A game based on the published ELLX procedures, simplified and more forgiving than real operations. Not for operational or training use. No wind. Radar picture updates every 4 seconds; pilots read back at once and act 4 seconds later.

0 points

HOW TO PLAY

Fast clearances. Click a field in the label: the CFL for a level list, the heading field (H270 or XFL) for left/right turns and direct-to, the speed field (4th line) for speeds, the runway to clear the ILS (a second click hands an established arrival to TWR). Drag from the aircraft to give a heading; release on a fix for direct-to. Click the callsign for the full menu. Drag a label to move it. A white box means the pilot is waiting for you.

Keyboard: select an aircraft, type and press Enter: H270, L270, R270, F110, A40, S200, S (own speed), D BREDI, I (ILS), T (transfer), X (request higher XFL). Esc closes.

Arrivals (green) call in descending on their STAR and fly the RNAV transition on their own. Shorten the route with headings or direct-to, descend them (the shading shows the minimum vectoring altitude: light 3000 ft, dark 4000 ft, TILVI area 5000 ft), then CLEARED ILS. They keep their level on the localiser and intercept the glidepath from below: 3000 ft at about 6 NM, 4000 ft at about 9 NM, 5000 ft at about 12 NM. Once established on the localiser and glidepath, hand them to TWR 118.105, ideally between 12 and 4 NM final.

Departures (blue) call when airborne, climbing to 4000 ft on their SID. Climb them to their exit level (XFL in the menu). If you need more room, REQ HIGHER XFL asks the next unit; the answer comes after 10 s (Brussels FL180, Langen FL160, Paris FL240, Strasbourg FL190) and the new level appears in the level lists and TRANSFER before they leave the TMA. Traffic leaving to the south (GTQ, N852) may be kept until 25 NM south of AKELU. After transfer the next unit climbs them to their cruising level (RFL in the menu). GTQ departures should reach FL120 before LX772 (RWY 06) or LX896 (RWY 24), as in the Strasbourg LoA, or be recleared FL100 (no penalty in the game).

Transits (yellow) cross at their level. Keep them clear of your traffic and transfer before they leave (southbound: until 25 NM south of AKELU).

Separation: 3 NM or 1000 ft. On final, wake spacing applies: 4 NM H behind H, 5 NM M behind H, 6 NM L behind H, 5 NM L behind M.

Race mode: every player gets the same traffic for a level and runway. The clock runs until all traffic is landed or transferred. Any separation or wake infringement stops the race. Other faults add time (1.2 s per penalty point, e.g. 60 s for an MRVA infringement). Fastest wins.

Traffic from the east and south shows up faded about 30 NM before the TMA. It is not yet on your frequency and calls in at the usual point.

Range and bearing: switch on R&B, then drag from an aircraft, a fix or any point to another. Lines on aircraft follow them; with one aircraft the line also shows the time to go. Click a line's label to delete it; CLR deletes all.

Headings are magnetic. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
